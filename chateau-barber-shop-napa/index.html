Our story

A downtown Napa institution.

Chateau Barber Shop was previously owned by Phil Enlow of Napa. In 2004 he sold the business to Santysouk "San" Yankee, and in 2013 San brought on Napa native Giancarlo "G" Fradella.

The specialties are precision haircuts, beards, straight-razor shaves and line work. No appointments: walk in, grab a seat, and become part of the family.
