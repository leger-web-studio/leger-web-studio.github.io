FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Romack the same as Rowmack Inc.?

Yes. Owner Robert "Bob" Mackert's business appears as Romack Home Maintenance Services on Google and as Rowmack Inc. on Yelp and in reviews.

What kinds of jobs do you take?

Sheetrock repair and texturing, doors, laminate flooring and baseboards, furniture assembly, carpentry, dry rot repair, caulking, plumbing fixture swaps, ceiling fans and appliance installs. If it involves wood and/or houses, ask.

How quickly can you come out?

Often fast. Reviewers describe same-day responses, and visits within an hour or an hour and a half.

How much does it cost?

Prices aren't posted online. Customers describe the pricing as fair, affordable and upfront. Call or text (707) 317-8126 to describe your project.

What are your hours?

Monday–Friday 7 AM–6 PM and Saturday 9 AM–3 PM. Closed Sunday.

Do you take credit cards?

Yes, credit cards are accepted.

How much experience do you have?

Bob says he has over 25 years of experience.
