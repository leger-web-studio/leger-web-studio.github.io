The salon

Clean, friendly, and relaxing.

Napa Valley Nails Salon is a full-service nail salon at 706 Trancas Street in Napa, doing manicures, pedicures, gel, dip powder, acrylics, extensions and nail art for both women and men.

Reviewers describe the salon as clean and hygienic with a huge variety of colors, and they keep coming back for relaxing pedicures, foot massages and thin, natural-looking acrylics. Regulars mention techs by name, including Ann, Lee, Joe, Kenny, Kevin and Kim.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, and regulars say the team can almost always fit them in at the last minute. If you'd like a specific tech, a reviewer recommends calling ahead at (707) 252-4551.

What services do you offer?

Manicures, pedicures, gel nails, dip powder, acrylics, gel and regular nail extensions, nail art and polish, plus men's manicures and pedicures. Waxing and skin care are also listed.

What are your hours?

Monday–Friday 9:30 AM–6:30 PM, Saturday 9:30 AM–6 PM. Closed Sunday.

Where are you located?

706 Trancas St, Suite A, Napa, CA 94558, with private lot parking.

Do you take credit cards?

Yes, credit cards are accepted.

How much do services cost?

Prices aren't posted online. Reviewers describe the pricing as very reasonable. Call (707) 252-4551 for current prices.
