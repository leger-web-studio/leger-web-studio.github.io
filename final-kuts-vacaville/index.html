FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I walk in?

Yes. Reviewers mention walk-ins, and one Google reviewer’s 1-year-old got a walk-in cut. To skip the wait, book your barber on Booksy.

How do I book a specific barber?

Each barber has their own Booksy page: Hollywood The Barber, Patrick, Marethebarber and Luis. Use the Book buttons above.

How much is a haircut?

On Booksy, a standard men’s haircut is $40–$45 depending on the barber, as of October 2026. Check Booksy for current prices.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. Kids’ cuts are $35–$50 on Booksy. Mare takes kids 8 and up, and Hollywood’s under-6 cuts aren’t available Thursday to Saturday.

Do you offer after-hours cuts?

Yes. Hollywood lists after-hours haircuts at $70+, and Patrick lists after/before-hours cuts at $70 on Booksy.

Do you take cards?

Patrick’s Booksy page lists credit cards as accepted. Call (707) 685-9136 to confirm for other barbers.

What are the hours?

Mon 10 AM–6 PM, Tue–Fri 10 AM–7 PM, Sat 9 AM–6 PM, closed Sunday, per Google. Other directories list slightly different hours, so check Booksy or call.
