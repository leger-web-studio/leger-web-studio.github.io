The shop

Good vibes on Merchant Street.

Final Kuts is a downtown Vacaville barbershop with a 5.0 rating across more than 200 Google reviews. Customers talk about the lively atmosphere, the conversations, and barbers who take their time.

Bring the kids. Reviewers keep mentioning how patient the barbers are with children, from first haircuts to fresh fades. Walk in, or pick your barber and book a time on Booksy.
