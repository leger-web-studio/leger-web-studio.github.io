FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an appointment?

You can book any barber online on Booksy. Per Booksy, Sundays are appointments only. Weekday and Saturday walk-in hours are listed on Google.

How much is a haircut?

On Booksy, a standard haircut runs $40–$50 depending on the barber, as of October 2026. Check Booksy for current prices.

Do you offer senior or military discounts?

Several barbers list senior (65+) cuts at $30 and military/first responder cuts at $35–$40 on Booksy.

Do you do hot towel shaves?

Yes. MartyTheBarber lists a traditional shave at $40 and haircut plus traditional shave with hot towel at $75. Viviana and Joe also list traditional razor shaves.

Do you take cards?

Yes, per the shop’s Yelp listing.

Is there Wi-Fi and accessible access?

Yes. Yelp and Booksy list free Wi-Fi and wheelchair accessibility.

What are the hours?

Mon–Fri 9 AM–6 PM and Sat 9 AM–3 PM per Google. Sundays are by appointment only per Booksy.
