The shop

No rush. Just a great cut.

C's Cuts (you'll also see it listed as C's Barber Shop) has been cutting hair on North Texas Street since 2010. The specialties are fresh cuts, tapers, fades, crew cuts, trims and razor line-ups.

Reviewers keep saying the same thing about Chris, Christine and the crew: they take their time, pay attention to detail, and are easy to talk to. Plenty of reviews come from parents bringing their kids in too.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an appointment?

No. Yelp lists C's Cuts as not appointment-only, so you can walk in. One Google reviewer also mentions getting an appointment when needed. Call (707) 673-7601 to check the wait.

Do you take credit cards?

No. Payment is cash or Zelle. Yelp lists the shop as not accepting credit cards.

What are the hours?

Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 5 PM. Closed Sunday. These are the Google listing hours; some directories show later closing times, so call ahead.

Where do I park?

There's a private parking lot (per Yelp). A Google reviewer notes the shop has two entrances, one in front and one in back.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. Lots of reviews come from parents bringing their sons in.

How long has the shop been open?

Since 2010, per the shop's Yelp listing.

Is the shop wheelchair accessible?

Yes, per Yelp.
